4. Summary of Our Core Data Practices

This summary is for convenience only. The full Policy governs.

  • Your browsing stays on your Device by default. Browsing Data and Vault Data are Local Data. We do not receive them.
  • Your passwords never leave your Device. Vault Data is encrypted on-device with AES-GCM using keys protected by the Android Keystore and unlocked by your device biometric or PIN.
  • Diagnostics are opt-in. We receive Diagnostics Data only if you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • We do not sell your Personal Data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising (Section 9).
  • We may use Aggregated Data and De-identified Data for any lawful purpose (Section 7).
